From t2000_limit take perJobUsdc, dailyUsdc and askAboveUsdc.

Run these five checks. If ANY fails, stop, tell me which one and by how
much, and do not post.

  TOTAL = 0.20 × 10 = 2.00

  1. TOTAL      2.00  <=  spendableUsdc
  2. maxUsdc    0.20  <=  perJobUsdc
  3. TOTAL      2.00  <=  dailyUsdc
  4. maxUsdc    0.20  <   askAboveUsdc
  5. TOTAL      2.00  <   askAboveUsdc

Check 2 is against maxUsdc, NOT the total. The per-job limit caps what
one job may cost, not what the batch costs. Checks 1 and 3 are the ones
that cap the batch.

https://t2000.ai/manage/connections and stop. You cannot raise it
